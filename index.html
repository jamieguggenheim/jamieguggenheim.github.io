Jamie Guggenheim

I am a Royal Society University Research Fellow and Birmingham Fellow at the University of Birmingham, working across engineering and cardiovascular sciences. I am also an Honorary Senior Research Fellow at UCL.

My research brings together optics, ultrasound and computational imaging to develop new ways of seeing inside biological tissue. I am particularly interested in optical sensing and light-control methods that enable new photoacoustic imaging capabilities.

I co-lead the Birmingham Photoacoustic Group, where we develop photoacoustic imaging methods and related optical and ultrasound technologies, connecting fundamental imaging science, device development and biomedical applications.
