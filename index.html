Webpage of Jamie Guggenheim

I am a Royal Society University Research Fellow working across engineering and medical sciences at the University of Birmingham, with an Honorary appointment at UCL.

I co-lead the Birmingham Photoacoustic Group, which develops photoacoustic imaging methods and related optical and ultrasonic technologies, connecting fundamental imaging science, device development and biomedical applications.
