Research

My research focuses on photoacoustic imaging, optical ultrasound sensing, and the control and modelling of light in complex media. We develop the underlying methods and devices that determine imaging sensitivity, resolution and the form that an imaging system can take.

Photoacoustic imaging uses short pulses of light to generate ultrasound within tissue. By detecting these sound waves, we can image optical absorption in structures such as blood vessels. Our interests span sensitive optical detectors, wavefront shaping, computational optics and miniature fibre-optic imaging systems.

Selected papers

For a full publication list, see Google Scholar.

  1. J. A. Guggenheim, D. M. Marques, T. J. Allen, O. O. Ogunlade and P. C. Beard
    Noise-equivalent pressures of ultrasound detectors used in photoacoustic tomography
    Nature Reviews Methods Primers 6, 59 (2026).

    A review comparing ultrasound detector sensitivity and its implications for photoacoustic imaging system design.

  2. B. Keenlyside, A. A.B., D. M. Marques, I. M. Vellekoop, J. A. Guggenheim.
    Sub-acoustic resolution photoacoustic imaging through scattering layers using speckle correlations
    arXiv:2604.24168 (2026). Preprint.

    Proof-of-concept imaging through a scattering layer using speckle correlations to resolve structures below the acoustic diffraction limit.

  3. B. Keenlyside, D. Marques, D. B. Phillips, E. Zhang, P. Beard and J. A. Guggenheim
    High-resolution imaging of ultrasonic fields through a multimode optical fiber
    Physical Review Applied 24, 054043 (2025).

    Combining wavefront shaping and optical ultrasound sensing to map ultrasonic fields through a single multimode fibre.

  4. J. Bewick, P. Munro, S. Arridge and J. A. Guggenheim
    Simulating light transmission and wavefront shaping in turbid media with a T-matrix method
    Optics Express 33, 52202–52218 (2025).

    A computational method for modelling coherent light propagation and wavefront shaping in scattering media.

  5. J. A. Guggenheim, J. Li, T. J. Allen, R. J. Colchester, S. Noimark, O. Ogunlade, I. P. Parkin, I. Papakonstantinou, A. Desjardins, E. Z. Zhang and P. C. Beard
    Ultra-sensitive planoconcave optical microresonators for ultrasound sensing
    Nature Photonics 11, 714–719 (2017).

    Optical microresonator ultrasound sensors combining high sensitivity with broadband response, demonstrated in ultrasound and photoacoustic imaging.
